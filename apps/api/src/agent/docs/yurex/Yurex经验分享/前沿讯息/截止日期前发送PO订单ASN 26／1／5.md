# 飞书文档：截止日期前发送PO订单ASN 26/1/5


## 翻译

**请在截止日期前发送采购订单中商品的预发货通知 (ASN)，以免订单被取消**

自 **2026 年 1 月 5 日**起，您的采购订单中未提供预先发货通知 (ASN) 的单个商品项目将在采购订单取消截止日期自动取消。

取消截止日期是指您必须向亚马逊确认发货意向的日期。如果在此日期前未收到确认，采购订单将被取消。

为确保订单处理顺利，我们要求您在截止日期前为采购订单中的每个商品发送 ASN。

此变更适用于新订单和现有订单。生鲜食品、设备和积压订单除外。

要跟踪订单状态，请在供应商中心执行以下步骤：

1. 在下拉菜单中选择“订单”，然后选择“采购订单”。
2. 点击“管理采购订单”或“最近修改的采购订单”选项卡即可查看您的采购订单。

## 原文

**Send ASNs for items in purchase orders ahead of deadline to avoid cancelation**

Effective **January 5, 2026**, individual line items in your purchase orders without an advanced shipment notification (ASN) will be automatically canceled on the purchase order cancellation deadline.

The cancellation deadline is the date by which you must confirm your intent to ship to Amazon. If confirmation is not received by this date, the purchase order will be canceled.

To ensure proper order processing, we require that you send an ASN for each item in the purchase order by the deadline.

The change applies to new and existing purchase orders. Grocery, Fresh, Devices, and Back-order purchase orders are excluded.

To track the status of your orders, follow these steps in Vendor Center

1. Select Orders in the drop-down menu, and then select Purchase orders.
2. Click either the Manage POs or the Recently modified POs tabs to view your purchase orders.
