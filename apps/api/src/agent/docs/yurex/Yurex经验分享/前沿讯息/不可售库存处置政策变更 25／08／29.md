不可售库存处置政策变更 25/08/29

## 翻译

1. **核心变更：** 针对违规、缺陷或召回等不可售商品，默认处置方式已更改为“按成本退回”（费用自理），2025/8/13 生效。

 - **如需弃置：** 请联系您的亚马逊对接人修改默认设置。
 - **注意：** 亚马逊不提供“认证销毁”服务。如需此证明，请务必选择退回自行处理。
2. **处理流程：**

退回前我们会发送邮件确认，请在 **7 天内** 回复：首选方式（弃置或退回）及有效的美国收货地址。

- **逾期后果：** 若未及时回复，我们将强制弃置商品，费用由您承担。

1. **立即行动：**

请前往 **Settings > Return Address** 检查并更新退货地址。

## 原文

**Update to our processes for unsellable inventory**

We’ve made an important change to the disposition of products we source from you that are unsellable because they don’t meet our policies—for example, if they’re prohibited, pose a safety risk, are defective, or have been recalled. On August 13, 2025, the default disposition setting for unsellable products was set to **return at platform purchase price**.

This means that unless you change the default setting, we will return the products to you and charge you the price of the return.

If you’d like to change the default disposition setting (for example, if you want the products disposed of by us at your expense instead of having them returned), work with your vendor manager or Amazon contact to do so.

**Note:** We do not offer certified-destruction services. If you require certified destruction, the products will be returned to you to manage.

Before we initiate a return of these products, we’ll send you an email that requests the following:

- Your preferred disposition: either dispose of or return at your own expense
- A valid US address that can receive the returns

You'll have seven days to respond to the email. If you do not respond within seven days, we will dispose of the products at your expense.
