# 飞书文档：锂离子电池产品空运限制的政策更新 25/12/31


## 翻译

**锂离子电池产品空运限制的政策更新**

为符合 ICAO 航空安全规定，自 **2026 年 1 月 1 日**起：

1. 适用范围： 所有含锂电池商品（包括电动工具、玩具等，含未安装的可拆卸电池）。
2. 技术指标： 额定能量 >2.7Wh 的电池，包装发货时充电状态 (SoC) 必须 ≤30%。
3. 行动指南： 请在 12 月 31 日 前在“产品合规”版块完成申报。
4. 影响提示： 逾期未申报将限制空运服务，仅支持地面运输，配送时效及覆盖范围将受损。

## 原文

**New requirement for products with lithium-ion batteries shipped by air**

Effective **January 1, 2026**, requirements for products packaged with lithium-ion batteries will be updated to comply with the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) and to ensure air transportation safety.

Per our Vendor Terms and Conditions, you’re required to provide accurate and complete product information for each product you offer. If you sell products that ship with a lithium-ion battery, you must provide the requested battery compliance information by December 31, 2025, to ensure your products can continue to be shipped by air.

To list products packaged with lithium-ion batteries, you must complete the battery compliance questions asked when you list or re-list products. Specifically, lithium-ion batteries with a watt-hour rating exceeding 2.7 Wh that are packed with products must have a 30% or less state of charge. If we’re unable to confirm whether your product complies with the state of charge requirement, your product will only be shipped by ground transportation until further notice.

To learn more about the battery compliance questions, go to [Dangerous goods (hazmat) required information and documentation](https://vendorcentral.amazon.com/hz/vendor/members/support/help/node/GQT3Y36MHND8L2G7?ref=vnlp_at_GEZME3NBJT5BTLLW_en-US_vnlnk_1).

For more information about lithium batteries, go to [Requirements for lithium batteries and products powered by lithium batteries](https://vendorcentral.amazon.com/hz/vendor/members/support/help/node/GRS4N7LVUJZTA7QN?ref=vnlp_at_GEZME3NBJT5BTLLW_en-US_vnlnk_2).
